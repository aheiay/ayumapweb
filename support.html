Support

Common questions answered below. If you need anything else, just drop us an email.

How do I import my past walks from Apple Health?

Open Ayumap and go to the Record tab. Tap "Import from Apple Health" and grant permission when prompted. Ayumap will pull in all walks and hikes automatically. Runs are excluded by design. Walks under 45 minutes are also filtered out — this keeps your map meaningful and avoids cluttering it with quick trips like a supermarket run.

How do I import GPX files?

Go to the Record tab and tap "Import GPX". You can select multiple files at once from Files, iCloud Drive, or any other app that supports the share sheet. Ayumap supports both GPX and FIT formats. If a third-party app has its own GPX export function, you can also tap Share within that app and look for the Ayumap icon in the share sheet — this will import that single file directly into Ayumap.

Why are some short walks not showing on my map?

By default, Ayumap filters out walks under 45 minutes when syncing from Apple Health — to keep your map meaningful rather than cluttered with every brief trip. You can adjust this threshold in Settings.

What is the difference between the free and paid plan?

The free plan gives you a full heatmap and path lines for one country of your choice — with no time limit on the map. Journal access is limited to your most recent 30 days. The Premium plan unlocks worldwide map access and your full journal history across all time. See the pricing section on the home page for full details.

Is my data private? Who can see my walks?

Only you. All personal GPS data is stored in a private iCloud container tied to your Apple ID — the developer cannot access it. If you participate in the optional community heatmap, only anonymous grid density is contributed. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I change my selected country?

Go to Settings and tap "Map Region". You can select a different country at any time. Your heatmap and path lines will update to show that country.

How do I delete a walk or journal entry?

Tap the three-dot button (•••) on any journal entry to reveal the delete option. You can also open the entry and tap the delete button at the very bottom of the screen. Deleted entries are removed from your iCloud container permanently.

Will my data transfer if I get a new iPhone?

Yes. All data syncs via iCloud automatically. Sign in with the same Apple ID on your new device, install Ayumap, and everything will restore.
