03

Not a tracking app.
A memory map.

Ayumap is designed to sit quietly alongside the tools you already use. Your Apple Watch, Apple Health, Garmin, or Strava — they handle the tracking. Ayumap handles the remembering.

⌚
Apple Watch

When you record a walk or hike with Apple Watch, Ayumap picks it up automatically. No tapping, no setup. Runs, commutes, and daily errands are intentionally ignored — only meaningful journeys make it to your map.

📁
GPX & FIT import

Bring in routes from Komoot, AllTrails, Garmin, or Strava. A journey recorded anywhere can become part of your personal map.

⏺
In-app recorder

For moments when you don't have your Watch — a quiet fallback. Start it, walk, forget it's running. The path will be there when you're done.

However the journey was recorded, it ends up in the same place — your map.

03

Private inside.
Shareable outside.

Sign in with Apple ID — nothing extra to create or remember. Your journeys live in your own private iCloud container, a quiet archive only you can access.

  • 🔒
    Sign in with Apple ID — nothing elseNo separate account, no email, no password. Your data is bound to your Apple ID via iCloud.
  • ☁️
    Private iCloud containerAll personal GPS data lives in a container only you can access. The developer cannot read it.
  • 🙈
    Anonymous community featuresThe optional community heatmap uses an anonymous device token only — no name, no email. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.
  • 📍
    Location: on request only"Always on" permission is only requested when you explicitly enable the background recorder.

The name

Ayumi (歩み) + Map

Ayumi means walking, steps, journey in Japanese. Combined with map, the name represents a personal map shaped by your journeys — a quiet record of where life happened, only yours.
