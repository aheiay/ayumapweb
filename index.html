02

Simple to use.
Nothing unnecessary.

Whether it is a travel memory, a mountain trail, a familiar walk near home, or a city exploration — Ayumap quietly collects it all. Import once, record going forward, revisit whenever.

What's included
  • ✓
    Heatmap — 1 country, all paths, foreverYour chosen country shows every path you've ever walked — no time limit on the map. Upgrade to unlock the whole world.
  • ✓
    Apple Health syncImport past walks automatically from Health. No manual work needed.
  • ✓
    GPX importBring in data from Komoot, AllTrails, Garmin, or Strava.
  • ✓
    Quiet background recorderWalks and meaningful journeys only. Short unimportant trips filtered out automatically.
  • ✓
    Reflective journalA private space for each walk — distance, elevation, time, and your own notes. Free plan shows the last 30 days; upgrade for full history.
  • ✓
    iCloud syncEverything syncs privately across your devices. No server sees your data.
Four tabs. No clutter.
🗺 Map
Full-screen heatmap and path lines. Stats panel slides up from the bottom. Share button top-right.
⏺ Record
Start / stop, live path preview, import from Health or GPX. All data sources in one place.
📓 Journal
Date-ordered private notes, each linked to that day's walk. Calm and quiet by design.
⚙️ Settings
Map style, recording mode, notifications, data sources. Everything in one honest place.
03

Private inside.
Shareable outside.

No account. No sign-up. No email. Your journeys live in your own private iCloud container — a quiet archive only you can access.

  • 🔒
    No account, no sign-up — everBound to your Apple ID via iCloud. Nothing to create or remember.
  • ☁️
    Private iCloud containerAll personal GPS data lives in a container only you can access. The developer cannot read it.
  • 🙈
    Anonymous community featuresThe optional community heatmap uses an anonymous device token only — no name, no email.
  • 📷
    Photos: read-onlyPhotoKit reads GPS coordinates from your photos. No images are copied or stored.
  • 📍
    Location: on request only"Always on" permission is only requested when you explicitly enable the background recorder.

The name

Ayumi means walking, steps, journey in Japanese. Combined with map, the name represents a personal map shaped by your journeys — a quiet record of where life happened, only yours.
